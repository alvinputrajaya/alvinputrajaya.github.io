The Brief

Making recycling something people want to be part of.

Waste is often seen as a problem, not an opportunity. We identified a simple shift: recycling becomes more meaningful when people immediately experience its value.

WAHU transforms everyday recycling into something people want to be part of. Through an optimistic brand system, every interaction reinforces one idea: small actions can create meaningful value for people, communities, and the environment.
