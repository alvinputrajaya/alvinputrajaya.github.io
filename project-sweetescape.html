The Brief

Nine years. Every stage of the business.

From 2016 to 2025 I led SweetEscape's creative department as Head of Creative, building and running a team of 12 that turned marketing strategy into campaign visuals.

Our core role was supporting the Marketing team. Every year we planned and executed the annual creative marketing campaign, and as the company moved through different stages of its business, the creative evolved with it.
